# Vocabulary and register

Read this file when you write to a supplier, a DMC or a host agency, or when a trade term in the request is unclear.

*Built from written trade sources. To be enriched after practitioner interviews.*

### Core glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Travel planner / travel designer | Independent professional who designs a tailor-made trip and charges for the advice. The client books directly, unless a licensed sales setup applies. |
| Travel agent / travel advisor | Professional who sells travel services or packages and is liable for them. A regulated activity in most countries. |
| DMC (destination management company) / ground handler | Company based in the destination that runs on-site logistics (guides, transfers, activities). |
| Host agency | Licensed agency that an independent planner works through so that client bookings can be made. |
| Discovery call | First conversation (30 to 45 min) where the planner gathers needs, constraints, budget and what the client dislikes. |
| Planning fee | Payment for design time, invoiced to the client. Distinct from a commission paid by a supplier. |
| Commission | Percentage paid by a supplier on a booking. Disclose it to the client when it exists. |
| Quote request | Price request sent to a DMC or supplier, with dates, number of travellers and the services wanted. |
| Plan B | The alternative planned for a day: weather, closure, fatigue. |
| Direct flight / non-stop flight | A direct flight keeps one flight number but can stop on the way. A non-stop flight does not stop. A frequent source of disputes. |

Country-specific notions (legal status, obligations, documents) belong in the country file: `references/france.md` for France.

### Register by reader

| Reader | Tone | Example phrasings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client | Warm, direct, choices owned, short sentences | "I recommend…", "I chose… because you told me…" |
| DMC, supplier | Factual, precise, complete on the first message | "Quote request: 2 adults, 2 children (4 and 7), 12 to 19 July, family room, half board." |
| Host agency | Contractual, with documents and references | "File for the Martin family, services to book: … Please send the cancellation terms for the client." |
