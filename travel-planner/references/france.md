# France: rules and notions specific to French travel planners

Read this file when the planner works in France or with French clients, before writing about bookings, fees or the planner's legal status. Summary checked on 7 Nov 2026. It is not legal advice: for any doubt, the reference texts are the Code du tourisme (Légifrance) and Atout France.

## Legal status: advice versus sale

- **Advice only.** A travel planner who designs the trip, invoices the advice and lets the client book directly does not need to be registered with Atout France.
- **Selling travel.** Registration (*immatriculation*) in the *registre des opérateurs de voyages et de séjours*, kept by Atout France, is mandatory for anyone who sells or organizes package holidays (*forfaits touristiques*), sells travel services they do not produce themselves, or facilitates linked travel arrangements (*prestations de voyage liées*).
- **Conditions of registration** (Code du tourisme, art. L211-18): a financial guarantee (*garantie financière*), which reimburses clients' payments and covers repatriation if the operator fails, and professional liability insurance (*assurance responsabilité civile professionnelle*). The registration number, the guarantor and the insurer must appear on the operator's website.

**Consequence for the writing.** Step 1 of `SKILL.md` sets the general rule on who books. In France, a planner can book only if they name their Atout France registration or the *agence de portage* that carries the sale. Without either, treat them as advising only: the deliverable gives booking links and no booking confirmation. Phrasings such as « réservé », « réservation confirmée », « notre offre », « notre forfait » or « réservez auprès de nous » present the planner as a seller.

## French notions

| Term | Meaning | Trunk equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immatriculation Atout France | Registration required to sell travel in France (see above). | Licence or registration to sell travel |
| Forfait touristique | Package holiday, as defined by the Code du tourisme, art. L211-2: [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000036242689). | Package |
| Agence de portage | Registered agency that carries the sale for an independent planner: bookings, financial guarantee, assistance. The planner acts as a business provider (*apporteur d'affaires*) or under the agency's umbrella. | Host agency |
| Carnet de voyage | In the trade, the document delivered to the client: day-by-day program, practical information, booking links. It does not mean the illustrated travel diary a traveller keeps. | Travel document |
| Honoraires de conseil | The planner's fee for design time, invoiced to the client. | Planning fee |
| Réceptif | Agency based in the destination that runs on-site logistics. | DMC / ground handler |
